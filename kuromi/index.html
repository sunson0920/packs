How to buy

A short path on Solana. Nothing here is a promise about price. There is nothing to swap until a contract address is published.

  1. 1

    Get SOL

    Buy Solana (SOL) on an exchange you already use, then withdraw it to a wallet you control. You need SOL for the swap and for the network fee.

  2. 2

    Open Phantom

    Install Phantom, or another Solana wallet you already trust. Write the recovery phrase on paper and keep it offline. Never share it.

  3. 3

    Swap once the CA is live

    When the contract address is published, paste it into a Solana DEX and swap SOL for $KUROMI. Until then the copy buttons on this page stay off, and there is no pool to trade.
