03 The order

How to buy on Solana.

Phantom or Solflare, some SOL, then a swap once the contract address is live. Until that address replaces TBA, there is nothing to trade.

  1. 1

    Get Phantom or Solflare

    Install Phantom or Solflare. Write the recovery phrase on paper and keep it offline. Never type it into a website, and never send it to someone who offers to help.

  2. 2

    Buy SOL

    Buy SOL on an exchange you already use, then send it to that wallet. You need SOL for the swap and for the Solana network fee.

  3. 3

    Paste the contract

    The contract on this page reads TBA. When it is a real address, copy it from here. Check it character by character before you paste it anywhere. A similar name is not the address.

  4. 4

    Swap on a Solana DEX

    Paste that address into Jupiter, Raydium, or pump.fun and swap SOL for the token. Until the contract is live, those sites have nothing of ours to trade. Ignore a direct message that offers a different address, or asks for your recovery phrase.
